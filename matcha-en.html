How to

Whisking basics

  1. Sift the matcha
    Sift about 2 g through the strainer to remove lumps.
  2. Pour the water
    Add 80 ml of water at about 80℃ (176℉).
  3. Whisk
    Start slowly to blend, then speed up.
  4. Done when fine foam covers the surface
    About 60 seconds is the guide.

Tips

Three tips for fine foam

  1. Always sift the matcha first
  2. Move your wrist in an “M” motion
  3. Don’t press the whisk against the bottom

Care

Caring for your set

Bowl & whisk holder
Wash by hand with a soft sponge and dry thoroughly.

Bamboo whisk
Rinse gently with water or lukewarm water, then set it on the holder and let it dry completely.

Scoop, spoon & strainer
Wash by hand and dry completely so no moisture remains.
